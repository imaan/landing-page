Sponsorship Activations · In development

Make more of the sponsorships you already have.

Your sponsored content already reaches people. What could you do to give them a better reason to get involved?

Sponsorship Activations is a tool I’m developing for people who already work with sponsorships: clubs, creators, brands and other organisations.

An example

A matchday ritual fans can join from anywhere.

Fictional club and partnership · an idea to test

The starting point
A small Portuguese club posts matchday photos featuring its bakery sponsor. New international followers like the club’s story, but have little reason to return between matches.
The activation
Make a short film about the baker who has fed supporters for years. Share a simple matchday recipe, then invite fans abroad to make it and show where they’re watching from.

“Same ritual. Different time zone. What does matchday look like where you are?”

Why the sponsor belongs
The bakery supplies the story, recipe and familiar face. Fans get a piece of the club’s local culture they can participate in, wherever they live.
Start small
Try one film and one matchday invitation. With permission, feature fan responses in the next instalment. Look for people joining in again, not just views.
Check first
Does the bakery want international attention? Can the club handle translations and fan submissions? Agree what would make the experiment worthwhile for both.
Where it is today

This is an early concept. I’m developing the approach; there isn’t an installable plugin yet.
